## Gate separation and no added criteria

G1: freeze versioned I01–I07 contracts, exact profile, checked source boundary and ABI semantics. G2: canonical strict CCAM decoder and concrete evaluator/refinement. G3: full-width native scalar results, trap/step precision, object and ABI verification. G4: managed heap, complete roots/safepoints, resource/authority and failure atomicity. G5: optional optimisations with validator and evidence binding. G6: actual S0–S3 native C-free self-hosting and transitive host dependency closure. G7: source-to-native refinement, security/capability/noninterference claims under stated threat model, performance/portability, certified artifact and release assurance. None may be represented as completed by a G0 acceptance. Missing release proofs and production C-free artifacts are **not G0 blockers** under CAP-004.
