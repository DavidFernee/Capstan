# Capstan ecosystem identity — provisional assets

These marks are **temporary repository placeholders**, not approved final logos and not a claim of trademark clearance or visual uniqueness.

## Direction

The working art direction is precision maritime engineering: navigation, structural boundaries and stable datums, rather than literal nautical clip-art.

- **Capstan** — controlled course, bearing and transformation.
- **Shroud** — a protected computational domain held by structural constraints.
- **Anchor** — a stable datum and explicit invariants.

## Files

- `capstan.svg`
- `shroud.svg`
- `anchor.svg`

The SVGs are deliberately simple, monochrome-first source assets. Their accent colours are secondary and may change. They should be replaced only after the identity is refined, tested at small sizes and reviewed for visual/trademark conflicts.

## Status and usage

Do not treat these as release branding. They are suitable only for provisional README/docs references and internal design discussion. Avoid embedding them in stable public APIs, binary resources or release artefacts until the identity is approved.

## Design constraints for the next iteration

1. Preserve a coherent family resemblance without making the marks interchangeable.
2. Avoid generic shields, gears, sails, literal anchors, infinity loops and stock compass roses.
3. Test silhouette recognition in monochrome at 16, 24 and 32 px.
4. Supply light/dark variants, clear-space rules, minimum sizes and accessible contrast.
5. Conduct a documented collision and trademark review before final adoption.
